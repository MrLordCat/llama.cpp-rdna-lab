## What was left alone, and why

- `examples/` — five of its directories are this fork's own probes
  (`vk_fp8_probe`, `dump_nextn`, `kv-precision-scout`, `attn-sparsity-scout`,
  `save-load-state`), and `examples/model-conversion` is the HF→GGUF path the
  quantisation work starts from. Removing the remaining upstream examples
  would save about 0.5 MiB and risk the two that matter.
- `gguf-py/` — wired into `pyrightconfig.json` and `ty.toml`, and it is the
  package `examples/model-conversion` and `examples/gguf` speak to.
- `tests/` — `test-backend-ops` and `test-quantize-fns` are how kernel and
  quant changes get checked here.
- `third_party/rocwmma` — the vendored fallback `ggml-hip/CMakeLists.txt`
  globs when ROCm's own include dir has no rocwmma; HIP 7.1 is still installed
  on purpose, so the fallback stays.
- `requirements/` — only 1 KiB in total, and two of the eight files are
  referenced (`requirements-tool_bench.txt` from the root `requirements.txt`,
  `requirements-model-tools.txt` from `tools/mtmd/requirements.txt`).
- `cmake/` toolchains — the arm64 and riscv64 files are named by
  `CMakePresets.json`, so deleting them means editing the presets too: more
  churn than the 5 KiB is worth.
- `docs/` — 497 of its 555 tracked files are this fork's own research and
  design notes, not upstream documentation.
